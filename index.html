1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the practices of BlueAppsBox ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") regarding your privacy while using our mobile application: Block Blast - Strawberry Puzzle Game (Package ID: com.blueapps.strawaberrypuzzle).

We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding your personal privacy. Our game provides delightful block-puzzle entertainment without requiring accounts, logins, or intrusive tracking.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Strict Privacy Guarantee: BlueAppsBox does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, financial info, or biometric data.

You can play all 300 levels entirely offline without providing any credentials or identity verifications.

3. Device-Local Storage & Gameplay Progression

To ensure you never lose your game progress, the app stores a small set of non-personal game variables directly on your personal device using local storage:

  • Current Highest Level Unlocked: Tracks your progression from Level 1 up to Level 300.
  • Personal High Score: Remembers your highest strawberry line-blast record.
  • Sound & Music Preferences: Stores whether you have enabled or muted chime effects.

This information remains exclusively on your device and is never uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free to play for everyone, Block Blast - Strawberry Puzzle Game utilizes the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show advertisements (banner ads, cold-start open ads, and optional rewarded video ads that give you an in-game board revive when out of moves).

Google AdMob Disclosures:

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device telemetry to deliver relevant, safe advertisements:

  • Advertising ID (GAID): Resettable advertising identifier provided by Android.
  • Device Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Ad load timestamps, interaction events, and crash logs.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Block Blast - Strawberry Puzzle Game is a family-friendly casual puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13. In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Ads served within the game comply with Google Play's Families and Child-Directed policies.
  • If you believe a child has inadvertently submitted personal information to us, please notify us at blueappsmanager@gmail.com, and we will take immediate corrective action.

6. Data Security & Retention

Since BlueAppsBox does not operate external databases that retain user personal data, there is no risk of your private credentials or personal data being breached or sold. Game progress data resides on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings.

7. User Rights & Controls

You maintain complete control over your advertising preferences on Android:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your device settings.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or Google Play Developer Policy updates. Any revisions will be published with an updated date.
